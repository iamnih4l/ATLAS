# Brag Plan: ATLAS — Your Life, Mapped

## What is this app?
ATLAS is a personal geographical experience journal that transforms an interactive 3D Earth into a living map of memories, adventures, and future aspirations.

## The angle
"This is my ATLAS. Where is yours?"
Most travel apps are generic check-in lists. ATLAS turns the globe into an intimate personal storybook of your life. Built by student founder Mohammed Nihal and deployed instantly to the world via Embarko.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
A warm cream canvas where a small pixel aeroplane flies across the screen, leaving a dotted trail that loops and morphs into the outline of the 3D Earth, revealing the ATLAS logo and tagline: "Your Life, Mapped."

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The Live 3D Globe**: Rotating Earth with glowing geo-pins and on-demand landmark photography.
2. **Memories & Automatic Dream List**: Seamless cross-device sync connecting Wishlist items directly into future adventures.
3. **The Embarko Launch**: Moving seamlessly from prompt to a live, permanent public URL at `https://atlas-app.embarko.app`.
4. **The Wow Factor**: Zooming from orbital space right into a captured memory with coordinates and photo.

## Outro / punchline
"This is my ATLAS. Where is yours?"
Bold call to action displaying the live URL `https://atlas-app.embarko.app` and `#DeployWithEmbarko` as the pixel aeroplane glides across the globe.

## User flow worth showing
1. Entry: Opening the interactive 3D globe orbiting in space.
2. Key Action: Tapping geographical pins to open memories and dream list destinations with real photos.
3. Result: An interconnected personal journal mapping your entire life's journey across the world.
