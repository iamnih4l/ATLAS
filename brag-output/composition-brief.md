# Hyperframes Composition Brief: ATLAS

## Objective
Create an authentic, cinematic 86-second vertical product demo film for ATLAS following the 6-part Embarko competition storyboard.

## Output
- Composition directory: `brag-output/composition/`
- Rendered video: `brag-output/brag.mp4`
- Format: vertical (9:16) — 1080x1920
- Duration: 86.0 seconds

## Source Material
- Project root: `atlas-app/`
- Live deployment: `https://atlas-app.embarko.app`
- Captured footage: Real high-resolution captures of the 3D Globe, pin detail modal, Memories list, Wishlist/Dream list, and Stats.
- App name: ATLAS
- Tagline: "Your Life, Mapped."

## Creative Direction
- Tone: Cinematic & personal founder narrative

- Middle highlights: Real app interactions (3D globe, geo-tagged pins, automatic dream list), prompt-to-live workflow on Embarko.
- Outro: "THIS IS MY ATLAS. WHERE IS YOURS?", live link `https://atlas-app.embarko.app`.
