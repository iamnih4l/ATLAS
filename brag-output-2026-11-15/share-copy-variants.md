## 3. Product Hunt / Hacker News / Reddit (Technical & Direct)
**Show HN: ATLAS — Personal geographical experience journal on a 3D Earth**

Hey everyone! We built ATLAS, a modern personal cartography journal built on Next.js 16, Three.js, Supabase PostGIS, and Google Gemini AI.

Key features:
- Interactive 3D Earth globe with tactile pop aesthetic
- Dual-pin system: Tomato Red for memories, Sunflower Yellow for bucket list dreams
- Photo GPS extraction: automatically parses EXIF metadata to pin memories accurately
- Orion AI companion: analyzes your travel history and recommends verified real-world adventures
- Offline guest mode via local cache persistence

Feedback and contributions welcome: https://github.com/iamnih4l/ATLAS
